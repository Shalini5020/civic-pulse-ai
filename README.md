# 🏛️ CivicPulse AI: Public Policy & Notice Auditor

**Track 1: Civic Pulse, Digital Governance & Public Information**

CivicPulse AI bridges the gap between complex government documentation and everyday citizens. Users can upload any government scheme PDF, RTI response, or mobile photo of a municipal notice and instantly receive a plain-language breakdown in **English or Hindi**—complete with voice read-aloud accessibility.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Multimodal Document Scanning:** Supports both digital PDFs and mobile photos (`JPG/PNG`) of printed government notices.
- **3-Flag Civic Audit:** Categorizes dense legal jargon into non-overlapping, actionable cards:
  - 🟢 **Citizen Benefits & Rights:** Subsidies, grants, and entitlements.
  - 🟡 **Eligibility & Documents:** Required IDs (Aadhaar, income certificates) and rules.
  - 🔴 **Hidden Traps & Deadlines:** Strict cut-off dates, penalties, and rejection risks.
- **Vernacular & Voice Accessibility:** Instant Hindi/English translation paired with one-click **Listen Aloud** audio mode for users with low reading literacy.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend & Deployment:** Python, Streamlit, Streamlit Community Cloud
- **AI Engine:** Google Gemini Multimodal API (`google-genai`)
- **Accessibility:** Web Speech Synthesis API
